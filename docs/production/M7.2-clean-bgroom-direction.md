# M7.2 — Clean BgRoom Direction

**Status:** OWNER VISUAL BASELINE APPROVED / BINARY PRODUCTION IMPORT PENDING  
**Project:** @Orlumi Studio → @Petal Nook  
**Date:** 2026-08-15

## Owner decision

The clean storybook-style BgRoom direction shown in the current approval round is accepted as the **v1 visual baseline**.

The base BgRoom must remain cleaner than earlier concepts and must not read as a conventional human home.

Lumie is not a human character, so Petal Nook should feel like a softly magical nook made for a tiny spirit rather than an adult living room, attic studio, bedroom or apartment.

## Area 2 rule — locked

The visible Lock Area is **not part of BgRoom**.

Area 2 must be a separate state-driven Godot visual component and is **hidden by default**.

The base BgRoom must show no:

- lock icon
- locked doorway
- dotted / dashed boundary
- Area 2 label
- blocked-path sign
- special locked-region glow

The right side only retains spatial capacity for a future Area 2 treatment.

A separate reusable scene now exists at:

`game/scenes/visuals/area2_overlay.tscn`

Its controller defaults to invisible and can only be displayed later when an explicit progression rule is approved.

## Approved visual language

Retain:

- soft storybook 2D
- creamy blush / warm oat atmosphere
- delicate flowers and stones
- natural wood floor
- gentle filtered light
- subtle glowing motes
- dreamy, quiet, slightly enchanted feeling
- generous open centre floor for gameplay objects

Decorative detail should stay mainly around the edges so Little Pot, furniture and Lumie remain the visual focus once layered into the scene.

## Non-human scale

Avoid major scene elements that establish an ordinary adult residence, including:

- sofa
- armchair
- adult bed
- office desk
- large bookshelf
- kitchen fittings
- realistic household storage
- conventional full-size human door as a focal point

The exact scale of the nook may remain gently ambiguous.

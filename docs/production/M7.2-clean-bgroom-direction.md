## Owner-approved opening room

The selected opening room is now the v1 visual baseline.

The room should feel like a **small natural woodland nook / raw tree-built shelter**, not a conventional human residence and not a large furnished cabin.

Locked visual characteristics:

- compact-feeling room scale
- bright, warm daylight
- creamy oat / pale wood / soft moss palette
- natural trunk, branch and root framing
- simple pale wall surface integrated into the tree structure
- left-side rounded window
- open centre floor for gameplay objects
- natural greenery only where it reads as part of the structure / environment
- dry, healthy, living wood rather than rotten or abandoned wood
- soft painterly storybook rendering

This colour and rendering treatment becomes the **Petal Nook v1 room-style reference** for subsequent item and companion integration.

## Opening state rule

The opening room remains intentionally bare.

Do not bake in:

- Curtain
- Wooden Rack / bookshelf
- lamp or decorative light fixture
- Small Table
- decorative furniture
- collectible props
- Little Pot
- Lumie or another companion
- Area 2 lock treatment
- UI, logo, text or labels

If an object can become a purchasable item later, it must remain a separate gameplay layer.

## Little Pot relationship

Little Pot appears from the beginning at **Lv1**, but remains a separate transparent gameplay asset.

Opening gameplay composition:

**Approved bare BgRoom + Little Pot Lv1 + gameplay UI**

The latest in-room scale/placement preview for Little Pot Lv1 was owner-approved on 2026-08-18.

## Area 2

Area 2 remains a separate state-driven component and is hidden at the beginning.

No visible lock marker, boundary, portal cue or locked-region glow is baked into the background.

## Design rule going forward

New Petal Nook items should inherit this room's visual language:

- warm natural materials
- low-to-medium saturation
- soft filtered sunlight
- painterly edges
- refined organic forms
- gentle magic rather than loud fantasy effects

The room should gain personality because the player purchases items, not because the background starts decorated.

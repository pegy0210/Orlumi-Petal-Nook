# M7.2 — Clean BgRoom Direction

**Status:** OWNER DIRECTION LOCKED / ART APPROVAL PENDING  
**Project:** @Orlumi Studio → @Petal Nook  
**Date:** 2026-08-14

## Owner correction

The base BgRoom should be **cleaner** than the recent concept attempts and should not read as a conventional human home.

Lumie is not a human character, so Petal Nook should feel like a softly magical nook made for a tiny spirit rather than an adult living room, attic studio, bedroom or apartment.

The previous visible lock treatment is **not part of BgRoom**. Area 2 will be introduced later as a separate state-driven visual layer.

## Reference direction retained

The approved direction retains these qualities from the earlier Petal Nook visual language:

- soft storybook 2D
- creamy blush / warm oat atmosphere
- delicate flowers and stones
- natural wood floor
- gentle filtered light
- subtle glowing motes
- dreamy, quiet, slightly enchanted feeling
- open centre floor for gameplay objects

## What changes

### Cleaner base scene

The next BgRoom should contain less permanent decoration and more breathing room.

Keep decorative detail mainly around:

- window edge
- lower corners
- wall/floor transition
- outer room edges

Keep the centre and lower-middle comparatively quiet so Little Pot, furniture and Lumie remain visually dominant once added as separate assets.

### Non-human scale

Avoid visual cues that strongly establish an ordinary adult human residence.

Do not use the following as major scene elements:

- sofa
- armchair
- adult bed
- office desk
- large bookshelf
- kitchen fittings
- realistic household storage
- conventional full-size door as a focal point

The exact scale of the nook may remain gently ambiguous.

### Area 2

The base BgRoom must show **no lock state at all**.

Do not bake in:

- lock icon
- locked doorway
- dotted / dashed boundary
- Area 2 label
- blocked-path sign
- glow indicating a locked region

The right side simply retains enough spatial capacity for a future Area 2 component.

Future Area 2 presentation will be a separate Godot layer and can appear only when product progression requires it.

## Next approval candidate requirements

The next BgRoom candidate should be:

- portrait
- 1280 × 2200 production target
- semi top-down / 3/4 perspective
- soft painterly storybook 2D
- left-side window
- clean top-left HUD zone
- generous centre/lower gameplay floor
- right side naturally open without a lock marker
- flowers / stones / greenery mainly around edges
- no Little Pot
- no Lumie
- no purchasable furniture
- no UI, logo or text
- no human-home staging

## Production rule

This document supersedes any earlier concept image that showed a baked-in lock area or conventional human-home treatment.

The next owner approval is for the **clean base BgRoom only**. Area 2 art is deferred.

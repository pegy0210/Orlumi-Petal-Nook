## Owner decision — opening room rule

The game begins with a deliberately **bare architectural shell**.

The opening room must not look already decorated or partly completed. Its sense of growth must come from items the player later buys and upgrades.

The previous richer room image is retained only as a **mood / later-completion reference**. It is **not** an approved opening-game background.

## What the opening BgRoom may contain

The base background may contain only non-purchasable architectural/environmental structure, such as:

- walls / room shell
- floor
- structural wood / trim
- built-in window opening
- soft ambient daylight
- restrained non-item atmospheric glow / motes
- minimal fixed architectural edging

The exact scale should remain gently ambiguous and suitable for a tiny spirit rather than an ordinary adult human residence.

## What the opening BgRoom must NOT contain

Do not bake in any item, furniture, décor, or progression reward, including:

- Curtain
- Wooden Rack / bookshelf
- lamp / decorative light fixture
- Small Table
- decorative pots / planters
- loose flowers used as room decoration
- cushions / rugs / seating
- shelves with ornaments
- framed pictures / wall décor
- collectible props
- Little Pot
- Lumie or another companion
- Area 2 lock treatment
- UI, logo, text, labels, dotted boundaries, or lock symbols

If an element could reasonably become a purchasable item later, it should not be part of the opening background.

## Little Pot exception

Little Pot **does appear from the beginning of gameplay at Lv1**, but it is a separate gameplay object layered above BgRoom.

Therefore:

**Opening visual = Bare BgRoom + Little Pot Lv1 + gameplay UI**

Little Pot must never be baked into `bg_room_main.png`.

## Area 2 rule — locked

Area 2 is not part of BgRoom and is hidden by default.

A separate reusable scene exists at:

`game/scenes/visuals/area2_overlay.tscn`

No Area 2 lock marker appears during the initial room state. Any later reveal is state-driven and must be approved separately.

## Opening visual language

The bare room should still feel Orlumi through:

- soft storybook 2D rendering
- creamy white / warm oat atmosphere
- gentle filtered light
- rounded, delicate architectural forms
- low-to-medium saturation
- quiet magical warmth
- open breathing space
- premium-cute refinement without looking furnished

It should feel **quiet, waiting, and not yet awakened**, rather than empty because art is missing.

## Non-human scale

Avoid visual cues that strongly establish an ordinary adult residence:

- sofa
- armchair
- adult bed
- office desk
- large household bookshelf
- kitchen fittings
- realistic storage furniture
- conventional full-size human door as the main focal point

The nook should feel naturally inhabitable by Lumie and future small companions.

## Progression principle

The room must visibly gain warmth and personality because the **player purchases items**, not because the base background already contains them.

Examples:

- Curtain appears only after purchase.
- Wooden Rack appears only after purchase.
- Small Table appears only after purchase.
- Future decorative lights or décor appear only if their gameplay/item state permits it.

## Acceptance gates

### Background

- 1280 × 2200 PNG
- environment only
- no furniture, Lumie, lock state, Area 2 marker, logo or UI baked in
- clean top-left HUD zone
- broad centre/lower movement space
- warm natural spirit-nook atmosphere

### Lumie

- star-ears formed by the ear silhouette itself
- glowing droplet tail
- soft luminous eyes
- creamy white / pastel lavender / blush pink / pale gold
- remains readable at roughly one-sixth of room height
- must not collapse into a generic bunny silhouette

### Furniture

- exact contract sizes from `assets/README.md`
- same structural identity across Lv1–Lv5
- progression adds cherished detail rather than clutter
- Small Table Lv4/Lv5 keeps the Little Pot centre slot clear
- Curtain aligns to the real window rather than floating as an isolated prop
- Wooden Rack props remain relevant to Petal Nook; no unrelated human clutter

## Runtime rule

Gameplay, economy, save state and unlock logic must remain independent of final-art presence. `VisualAssetService` continues to resolve final-path PNGs automatically and falls back safely when files are missing.

## CI rule

`tools/validate_assets.py` validates every final-path PNG that exists. Missing assets remain PENDING during production, while wrong dimensions or invalid PNG files fail CI.

## Owner review gate

Do not stop merely because an asset has been integrated. Raise an owner review only when the integrated MainRoom exposes a genuine aesthetic decision or contradicts an already approved direction.

# M7.4 — Lumie Visual Integration Gate

**Status:** USABLE V1 BASELINE ACCEPTED / FINAL POLISH DEFERRED  
**Project:** @Orlumi Studio → @Petal Nook  
**Date:** 2026-08-30

## Decision

Lumie now has a usable Petal Nook v1 baseline. The owner confirmed the current version is close enough to use, although it is not considered the final polished Brand Canon rendering.

The source of truth remains **@Orlumi Studio → Brand Canon → Lumie**. Petal Nook may not invent a substitute mascot.

## Canon — non-negotiable

- star-ears formed by the ear silhouette itself
- ear structure still reads as ears and retains inner-ear/cavity logic
- no decorative star stickers used as a substitute for star-ear construction
- glowing droplet tail
- soft luminous eyes
- creamy white, pastel lavender, blush pink and pale gold palette
- recognisable Brand Canon proportions and identity

## Petal Nook runtime scale

Owner direction:

- Lumie is deliberately smaller than earlier mockups
- the room reads larger around Lumie
- Lumie should be free to move broadly around the main floor
- the accepted approximate visual scale is the current enlarged-small-companion balance, not a human/avatar scale

Movement has been widened in `game/data/room_layout.gd` and now avoids owned furniture zones.

## Production asset contract

Folder:

`assets/companions/lumie/`

Required v1 files:

- `lumie_normal.png` — 220 × 220 transparent PNG
- `lumie_annoyed.png` — 220 × 220 transparent PNG
- `lumie_shadow.png` — 180 × 80 transparent PNG

A later Brand Canon polish pass may replace these binaries without changing gameplay code.

## Gameplay contract

Lumie appears after the first real purchase/upgrade:

- Little Pot reaches Lv2, OR
- Wooden Rack reaches Lv1, OR
- Curtain reaches Lv1, OR
- Small Table reaches Lv1

Lumie remains a visual companion and does not provide economy or Comfort bonuses.

## Final polish gate

Before public release artwork is considered final, re-check:

1. exact star-ear construction
2. droplet-tail attachment and glow
3. face/eye match to Brand Canon
4. normal and annoyed states are the same character
5. in-room scale remains small enough for broad movement

This polish gate does not block the current MainRoom / furniture / UI integration work.

# M7.4 — Lumie Visual Integration Gate

**Status:** ACTIVE — INTERNAL ART QA BEFORE OWNER REVIEW  
**Project:** @Orlumi Studio → @Petal Nook  
**Date:** 2026-08-18

## Objective

Integrate Lumie as the core Orlumi character anchor without turning Lumie into a generic bunny mascot.

Lumie appears only after the player's first real purchase or upgrade. Lumie is not present in the opening room state.

## Canon — non-negotiable

Lumie is a starlit bunny spirit born from little lights and wonder.

Primary identity:

- **star-ears are formed by the ear silhouette itself**
- the ears remain a pair of upright ears connected naturally to the head
- the star quality comes from soft angular/star-point structure in the ear shape, not decorative star stickers
- the ear structure must still read as an ear, including an inner ear/cavity area
- do not reduce the ears to detached star shapes or horn-like points

Secondary identity:

- glowing droplet tail
- soft luminous eyes
- soft pear / little-light body shape
- large head, small body, short limbs
- gentle non-human spirit proportions

Palette:

- creamy white
- pastel lavender
- blush pink
- pale gold

Keep the palette restrained, generally within about four major colour zones.

## Production asset contract

Folder:

`assets/companions/lumie/`

Required v1 files:

- `lumie_normal.png` — 220 × 220 transparent PNG
- `lumie_annoyed.png` — 220 × 220 transparent PNG
- `lumie_shadow.png` — 180 × 80 transparent PNG

Normal and annoyed must share the same body identity, apparent scale, anchor and camera.

## Petal Nook rendering adaptation

Lumie should match the current raw woodland-cabin / painterly Petal Nook environment rather than look pasted in from a separate glossy mascot sheet.

Use:

- soft painterly 2D rendering
- gentle cream/lavender body shading
- subtle warm rim light
- restrained glow
- readable silhouette at mobile scale

Avoid:

- hard vector outline
- glossy 3D toy surface
- excessive sparkle clutter
- costume-heavy design at v1 baseline
- cape/crown/wings as required identity features
- ordinary rabbit ears with star decorations attached
- detached star objects pretending to be ears
- ears that read as horns

## Normal state

Normal Lumie should communicate:

- gentle curiosity
- soft alertness
- calm companionship
- slight luminous presence

Default pose must be neutral enough to support random emoji reactions without requiring a separate full-body pose for each emoji.

## Annoyed state

Annoyed is not an aggressive redesign.

Keep:

- same silhouette and proportions
- same ears and tail
- same scale / anchor

Change primarily:

- eye / brow expression
- mouth
- subtle posture tension

The annoyed state lasts 30 seconds in gameplay and should read clearly at 220 × 220 without becoming scary or noisy.

## Shadow

`lumie_shadow.png` should be:

- soft oval / grounded contact shadow
- low contrast
- compatible with warm wooden floor
- separate from body sprite
- not a strong black blob

## Internal QA — mandatory before owner review

Do not show a candidate to the owner unless all checks pass:

- ears clearly read as Lumie's integrated star-ear structure
- star quality comes from ear silhouette, not decorations
- inner ear/cavity remains visible enough to read as an ear
- ears connect naturally into the head
- no accidental extra ear / head horn / top ornament
- droplet tail is present and visibly luminous
- eyes are soft luminous, not generic black mascot eyes
- body is pear-shaped / little-light-like with short limbs
- normal and annoyed are unmistakably the same character
- silhouette remains readable at 220 × 220
- painterly treatment fits the approved Petal Nook woodland-cabin environment
- no costume or accessory becomes necessary for character recognition

## Gameplay relationship

Lumie unlock trigger remains the first real purchase/upgrade:

- Little Pot reaches Lv2, OR
- Wooden Rack reaches Lv1, OR
- Curtain reaches Lv1, OR
- Small Table reaches Lv1

Lumie v1 is a visual companion only and does not modify economy or Comfort.

## Owner gate

After internal QA, present the owner with a compact Lumie v1 approval set showing at minimum:

1. Normal state
2. Annoyed state
3. clear star-ear construction
4. glowing droplet tail visibility
5. in-room scale check against the current Petal Nook environment

Only after owner approval should the final binaries enter the production asset paths.

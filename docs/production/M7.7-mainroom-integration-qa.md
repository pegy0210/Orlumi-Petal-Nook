## Objective

## Locked MainRoom layout

Owner selected **Layout A — central free-movement composition**.

Rules:

- portrait 1080 × 1920 reference canvas
- room reads larger around Lumie
- Lumie remains a small companion rather than a player-sized avatar
- broad central floor remains free
- furniture grows around room edges
- right edge retains future Area 2 capacity
- Area 2 lock/portal is not visible in the base room

Runtime coordinates are maintained in `game/data/room_layout.gd`.

## Furniture integration

- Curtain remains aligned with the left window and hidden at Lv0.
- Wooden Rack sits at an edge zone and does not constrict the central movement field.
- Small Table sits outside the main Lumie corridor.
- Little Pot begins on the floor.
- When Small Table becomes owned, Little Pot automatically moves onto the table-top slot.
- Table Lv4/Lv5 art must preserve the Little Pot centre clearance.

## Lumie movement

Lumie movement is widened to suit the larger-room composition.

Runtime now:

- uses the approved broad movement rectangle
- stays left of future Area 2 reserve
- rejects targets inside owned Wooden Rack / Small Table obstacle margins
- avoids the floor Little Pot before Small Table is owned
- remains tappable whenever standard gameplay input is available

## Petal Nook furniture art direction

Furniture should feel made for a tiny non-human spirit nook rather than an ordinary adult human house.

Use:

- natural raw wood / branch construction
- rounded handmade forms
- low bark-pattern density
- soft painterly 2D rendering
- warm pale wood, cream, restrained lavender/blush/gold accents
- small cherished details introduced gradually by level
- gentle magic mostly at higher levels

Avoid:

- conventional human furniture scale
- rectilinear mass-market shelving
- dark heavy timber
- dense grain or repeated bark texture
- excessive vines/leaves
- glossy toy plastic
- generic fantasy clutter

# M7.3 — Little Pot Integration Gate

**Status:** OWNER VISUAL APPROVED / BINARY PRODUCTION IMPORT PENDING  
**Project:** @Orlumi Studio → @Petal Nook  
**Date:** 2026-08-18

## Objective

Little Pot is the only gameplay item visible from the beginning of the game. It is layered above the bare Opening BgRoom and becomes the first item whose visual progression is calibrated against that room.

The pot must feel native to the Orlumi universe rather than like a generic houseplant prop.

## Gameplay contract

Little Pot starts visible at **Lv1**.

Paid upgrades:

- Lv1 → Lv2: 30 Petals
- Lv2 → Lv3: 60 Petals
- Lv3 → Lv4: 120 Petals
- Lv4 → Lv5: 240 Petals

It remains tappable at every level.

The first real upgrade, including Little Pot Lv1 → Lv2, may trigger Lumie's first appearance.

Little Pot is **not** a separate seed-growing minigame. It is an upgradeable item/furniture object inside the Petal Nook economy.

## Opening-room relationship

Opening visual state:

**Bare BgRoom + Little Pot Lv1 + gameplay UI**

Curtain, Wooden Rack, Small Table, lamps and other decoration are not present until their own item/progression rules make them appear.

Little Pot must remain a separate transparent asset and must not be baked into BgRoom.

## Production asset contract

Folder:

`assets/furniture/little_pot/`

Required files:

- `little_pot_lv1.png`
- `little_pot_lv2.png`
- `little_pot_lv3.png`
- `little_pot_lv4.png`
- `little_pot_lv5.png`

Each file:

- exact 280 × 280 PNG
- transparent background
- same canvas dimensions
- same ground / pot baseline
- same apparent camera angle
- consistent pot identity and footprint
- centred consistently enough that upgrades do not visually jump

CI validates all five production paths.

## Approved visual progression

Owner approved the latest Little Pot set on 2026-08-18.

Approved progression logic:

- **Lv1:** tiny two-leaf sprout
- **Lv2:** fuller green leaves
- **Lv3:** established plant with buds / first small bloom
- **Lv4:** clearly flowering
- **Lv5:** full mature bloom with restrained magical light detail

The visual progression is monotonic and keeps the same terracotta pot identity, camera angle and overall footprint.

## Layout baseline

Current reference rectangle in `game/data/room_layout.gd`:

`Rect2(385, 1135, 280, 280)`

Reference viewport: **1080 × 1920**.

Final anchor tuning occurs after the bare Opening BgRoom and approved Little Pot PNGs are physically integrated.

## Art direction locked

Little Pot should remain:

- tiny and cherished
- soft storybook 2D
- gently rounded
- warm terracotta / natural material
- premium cute, not toy-plastic
- compatible with raw woodland cabin environment
- quietly magical, with the strongest magical treatment reserved for Lv5

Avoid future edits that turn it into glossy plastic, highly saturated fantasy decor, or a human-household prop.

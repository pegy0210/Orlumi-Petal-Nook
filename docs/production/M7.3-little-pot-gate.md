# M7.3 — Little Pot Integration Gate

**Status:** ACTIVE — INTERNAL ART QA REQUIRED BEFORE OWNER REVIEW  
**Project:** @Orlumi Studio → @Petal Nook  
**Date:** 2026-08-15

## Objective

Little Pot is the only gameplay item visible from the beginning of the game. It is layered above the bare Opening BgRoom and becomes the first item whose visual progression is calibrated against that room.

The pot must feel native to the Orlumi universe rather than like a generic houseplant prop.

## Gameplay contract

Little Pot starts visible at **Lv1**.

Paid upgrades:

- Lv1 → Lv2: 30 Petals
- Lv2 → Lv3: 60 Petals
- Lv3 → Lv4: 120 Petals
- Lv4 → Lv5: 240 Petals

It remains tappable at every level.

The first real upgrade, including Little Pot Lv1 → Lv2, may trigger Lumie's first appearance.

Little Pot is **not** a separate seed-growing minigame. It is an upgradeable item/furniture object inside the Petal Nook economy.

## Opening-room relationship

Opening visual state:

**Bare BgRoom + Little Pot Lv1 + gameplay UI**

Curtain, Wooden Rack, Small Table, lamps and other decoration are not present until their own item/progression rules make them appear.

Little Pot must remain a separate transparent asset and must not be baked into BgRoom.

## Production asset contract

Folder:

`assets/furniture/little_pot/`

Required files:

- `little_pot_lv1.png`
- `little_pot_lv2.png`

- centred consistently enough that upgrades do not visually jump

CI validates all five production paths.

Current reference rectangle in `game/data/room_layout.gd`:

`Rect2(385, 1135, 280, 280)`

Reference viewport: **1080 × 1920**.

Final anchor tuning occurs only after the bare Opening BgRoom is approved and physically integrated.

## Art direction

Little Pot should feel:

- tiny and cherished
- soft storybook 2D
- gently rounded
- premium cute, not toy-plastic
- warm natural material
- compatible with creamy blush / warm oat / moss sage palette
- quietly magical without a loud fantasy effect

Avoid:

- generic supermarket flowerpot
- glossy plastic
- thick black outline
- high saturation
- oversized decorative clutter
- human-household realism

## Upgrade readability

Levels must progress monotonically. A later level must never look less developed than an earlier level.

Working progression:

- **Lv1:** tiny sprout; simplest intentional state
- **Lv2:** fuller leaves / stronger sprout
- **Lv3:** buds and at most a very small first bloom signal
- **Lv4:** clearly flowering and fuller than Lv3
- **Lv5:** mature v1 state; fullest bloom and strongest restrained Orlumi wonder

The pot body, camera, footprint and baseline remain consistent across all five levels.

Do not make each level look like an unrelated object.

## Internal QA gate — mandatory

Before any Little Pot set is shown to the owner, the Studio must internally check:

- Lv1 < Lv2 < Lv3 < Lv4 < Lv5 progression is visually monotonic
- flowers / buds never disappear at a later level
- plant volume does not regress
- pot identity does not change unexpectedly
- angle and baseline match across all five assets
- no unrelated mascot/character appears inside the plant
- no extra décor is introduced that implies another purchasable item
- mobile-scale silhouette remains readable
- the set still fits the bare Opening BgRoom style

If any check fails, regenerate/correct internally before presenting it.

## Objective

Little Pot is the first gameplay object shown from the beginning of the game, so it becomes the first approved furniture anchor against the clean BgRoom baseline.

The pot must feel native to the Orlumi universe rather than like a generic houseplant prop.

## Gameplay contract

Little Pot starts visible at **Lv1**.

Paid upgrades:

- Lv1 → Lv2: 30 Petals
- Lv2 → Lv3: 60 Petals
- Lv3 → Lv4: 120 Petals
- Lv4 → Lv5: 240 Petals

It remains tappable at every level.

The first real upgrade, including Little Pot Lv1 → Lv2, may trigger Lumie's first appearance.

## Layout baseline

Current reference rectangle in `game/data/room_layout.gd`:

`Rect2(385, 1135, 280, 280)`

This position is deliberately in the centre/lower gameplay floor and stays clear of the future Area 2 reserve.

Final anchor tuning occurs after the approved production BgRoom PNG is physically present in the repo, but the current baseline is safe for visual approval work.

## Art direction

Little Pot should feel:

- tiny and cherished
- soft storybook 2D
- gently rounded
- premium cute, not toy-plastic
- warm natural material
- compatible with creamy blush / warm oat / moss sage palette
- quietly magical without a loud fantasy effect

Avoid:

- generic supermarket flowerpot
- glossy plastic
- thick black outline
- high saturation
- oversized decorative clutter
- human-household realism

## Upgrade readability

Levels must visibly progress, but progression should stay refined.

Suggested progression logic:

- **Lv1:** tiny new sprout; simplest pot state
- **Lv2:** slightly fuller sprout / early leaves
- **Lv3:** recognizable growth milestone; may introduce first subtle magical or floral detail
- **Lv4:** fuller, more cherished presentation
- **Lv5:** mature v1 state with strongest but still restrained Orlumi wonder

The pot identity, silhouette and footprint should remain consistent across levels.

Do not make each level look like an unrelated object.

## Approval checklist

Owner approval should confirm:

- [ ] pot shape / material fits Orlumi
- [ ] Lv1 looks intentionally simple, not unfinished
- [ ] Lv2–Lv5 progression is clearly readable
- [ ] all levels feel like one object evolving
- [ ] silhouette remains clear at mobile scale
- [ ] no level becomes visually noisy
- [ ] perspective fits the approved BgRoom direction
- [ ] baseline and scale are consistent
- [ ] transparent separation from BgRoom is clean
- [ ] Lumie will not be visually overpowered by the pot

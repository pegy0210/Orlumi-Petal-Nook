## Visual-system rule

Each item is a separate transparent gameplay asset. Nothing is baked into `bg_room_main.png`.

At Lv0 the object is fully hidden.

For v1, visual progression may use three major art stages to control production scope:

- **Stage A:** Lv1–Lv2
- **Stage B:** Lv3–Lv4
- **Stage C:** Lv5

The gameplay level remains 1–5 even when two adjacent levels share the same base visual stage. Small glow/detail changes may differentiate adjacent levels if useful.

## Petal Nook room-style constraints

All items must fit the approved Opening BgRoom:

- warm creamy oat / pale natural wood / soft moss environment
- restrained surface texture
- low visual-pattern density
- sparse foliage
- no dense tiny repeated details
- bright filtered daylight
- organic, non-human-scale feeling
- soft painterly storybook 2D

Avoid making the room feel like a conventional human living room.

## Wooden Rack direction

The rack must feel hand-grown / hand-built from natural branches rather than polished factory furniture.

Use:

- rounded branch-like uprights
- simple open shelves
- pale warm wood
- modest asymmetry
- gentle handcrafted character

Avoid:

- modern bookshelf
- dense books as baked-in content
- heavy cabinetry
- dark varnished wood
- too many knots / bark stripes / repeated grooves

Lv progression should become slightly more refined, stable and useful, not simply larger.

## Curtain direction

Curtain is purchased separately and therefore **must not appear in the opening room**.

It should dress the existing left-side rounded window only after purchase.

Use:

- light airy textile
- soft lavender / blush-lavender
- small amount of pale-gold fastening detail
- minimal folds at mobile scale
- gentle semi-sheer feeling

Avoid:

- heavy drapes
- dense pleating
- loud lace patterns
- ornate human-palace styling

Lv progression may add fullness, trim refinement and subtle luminous thread at higher stages.

## Small Table direction

The Small Table should feel sized for Lumie / small companions, not adult human furniture.

Use:

- low rounded table
- natural wood top
- short sturdy legs
- organic edge
- compact footprint

Avoid:

- dining table proportions
- elaborate carved furniture
- tablecloth baked into Lv1 unless specifically part of the stage progression
- excessive decoration

Lv progression may add edge refinement, tiny trim details and a restrained magical finish at Lv5.

## Carpet / Rug status

Carpet is **not part of this gate**.

It is a future purchasable decor item and remains absent from the opening room.

## Internal QA — mandatory

Before presenting owner-facing candidates:

- item silhouette readable at mobile scale
- each item fits the current room perspective
- Lv0 hidden rule preserved
- visual progression never regresses
- texture/pattern density remains low
- no item introduces unrelated decor
- no dense leaves, lace, books, ornaments or micro-patterns
- natural materials feel dry, healthy and softly crafted
- item placement does not create a conventional human-room composition
- Curtain aligns with the approved left window
- Wooden Rack and Small Table stay inside their reserved layout regions
